Download

Get Brevio — free

One click to download. Setup takes under a minute.

macOS

macOS 11 to macOS 27 · Apple Silicon & Intel

  1. Click Download for Mac and open the Brevio app.
  2. If macOS says it can't verify the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Click Move to Applications. Your companion appears at the bottom of the screen; its icon is in the menu bar.
Download for Mac

No security prompt: paste this into Terminal instead

curl -fsSL https://abhiramupadrashta.github.io/Brevio/install.sh | bash

Windows

Windows 10 & 11 · 64-bit

  1. Click Download for Windows to get Brevio-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Brevio installs in seconds and starts. Its icon is in the system tray.

FAQ

Questions

Is Brevio free?

Yes. Brevio is completely free. If it makes you smile, you can support it.

Which companions are there?

Three glossy bubble buddies (Sky, Mint and Lilac), a cat, a dog, a sloth, Boo the see-through ghost and Puffy the fluffy cloud. Change it from the menu bar icon or Settings.

How does it know about notifications?

On Mac, Brevio notices when an app's Dock badge goes up (for example a new WhatsApp message or email). It never reads the message itself, and nothing leaves your computer. On Windows it reacts to its own reminders.

Will it get in my way?

No. Clicks pass straight through everywhere except the companion. Drag it anywhere, or hide it from the menu.

Does it use a lot of battery?

No. Brevio 2 is drawn with simple shapes instead of a 3D engine, so it's light on battery and memory.

How do I update?

Click the menu bar (or tray) icon → Check for Updates…. Brevio downloads the new version, installs it and restarts. It also checks automatically about once a day.

How do I uninstall?

Mac: quit from the menu and drag Brevio from Applications to the Trash. Windows: Settings → Apps → Brevio → Uninstall.
